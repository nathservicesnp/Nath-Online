Good to know

A few questions, answered.

Is Nath a government office?

No. Nath Online Services is an independent assistance provider. We do not issue government documents or guarantee approvals, appointments, or processing times.

Does every service cost Rs. 50?

No. Rs. 50 is the starting service charge. Your final quote depends on the steps, complexity, and time involved. Provider fees, government fees, bill amounts, and ticket fares are separate where applicable. We explain the charges before you proceed.

Can I complete everything from home?

Many steps can be supported remotely. Some services require personal attendance, biometrics, original-document checks, or action on an official portal. We will explain these requirements for your request.

How should I share documents?

Start by telling us which service you need. We will explain which details are necessary and agree how to provide them. This website does not accept document uploads. Do not send passwords, PINs, OTPs, or unnecessary identity documents.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Contact us as early as possible. We will explain what work has already been completed and whether any provider payment can be recovered. See our terms for cancellation and refund details.

What if my service is not listed?

Message the Founder/CEO on +977 9867302353. We will check whether assistance is available before accepting the request.

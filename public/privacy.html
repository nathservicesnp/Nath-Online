Nath Online Services

Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

About this policy

This policy covers www.nathonline.com.np and enquiries handled by Nath Online Services, Butwal, Rupandehi, Lumbini Province, Nepal. Contact the Founder/CEO at +977 9867302353 about privacy or your information.

When you browse

This website offers a service and careers enquiry form. It has no public account system, document uploads, advertising trackers, or analytics added by Nath. The guided assistant runs in your browser and does not send or store chat choices. The hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses, request times, and browser details for delivery, security, and operational logs.

When you contact us

If you submit the website form, we store your name, phone number, selected service or career interest, message, submission time, consent version, and follow-up status in Cloudflare D1. This is used to respond to your enquiry and manage follow-up. Only authorised Cloudflare account members can review records in the private dashboard. Cloudflare processes this data as the hosting/database provider and may store it outside Nepal. Your IP address is used temporarily for submission rate limiting, but is not stored in the enquiry record. If you call or use WhatsApp, we receive the contact details and messages you choose to share. For a requested service, we explain what additional information is needed and why before asking for it. Please provide only relevant details and share another person’s information only with appropriate authority.

Use and sharing

Information is used to respond, explain charges, carry out the service you authorise, and maintain necessary service or payment records. Relevant details may need to be submitted to the authority or provider involved in your request. We explain this before proceeding. We do not sell your personal information. Disclosures required by applicable law may also be necessary.

Documents and account security

Do not send passwords, wallet or banking PINs, OTPs, or unnecessary identity documents. You should enter credentials yourself on official portals. We limit handling to what the agreed service needs, but no communication channel is risk-free.

Retention and your requests

Enquiries should be reviewed regularly and removed when no longer needed. Our policy is to delete closed enquiries and career expressions of interest within 90 days unless you agree to continued follow-up or applicable legal obligations require retention. The team performs these reviews in the private dashboard. Cloudflare recovery backups may retain deleted data for its backup retention period. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, or to withdraw permission for future optional processing. We may need to verify your identity; required records and provider-held data may not be deletable by Nath. Ask about the retention period for your particular service before sharing documents.

External services and children

WhatsApp and any payment or government portals have their own privacy practices. Links open those services only when you choose them. A parent or guardian should arrange assistance involving a child’s personal information.

Policy changes

Updates will be posted here with a revised date. The actual service process must follow this policy and applicable Nepal law.
